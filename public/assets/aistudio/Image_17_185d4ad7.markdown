## Brand & Style

The design system embodies athletic precision, dynamic energy, and elite modern performance. Designed for high-performance fitness enthusiasts, runners, and calisthenics athletes, the interface communicates technical mastery, motivation, and clarity under rapid movement or low-light conditions.

The visual direction merges **Technical Dark Minimalism** with **Hyper-Dynamic Glassmorphism**. Dark graphite canvases eliminate visual glare, allowing vivid athletic luminescence—neon electric greens, vibrant cyans, and incendiary sports oranges—to guide visual priorities, real-time pace shifts, and metabolic milestones without friction. Every interaction feels snappy, weighted, and engineered like high-end performance telemetry gear.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict semantic roles over deeply stratified graphite backgrounds to ensure immediate parsing at a glance:

- **Deep Surfaces (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`, `#334155`):** The primary canvas begins at midnight graphite (`#0F172A`), lifting to surface slate (`#1E293B`) for container units and card panels, with bordered separation via slate-muted outlines (`#334155`).
- **Primary / Performance Neon (`#22C55E` & `#10B981`):** Represents vital progression, execution states, system health, cadence synchronization, and completion alerts.
- **Secondary / Metric Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** Assigned exclusively to telemetry, distance tracking, pace telemetry, GPS coordinates, and real-time biometric analysis.
- **Tertiary / Dynamic Flare (`#F97316`):** Reserved for explosive exertion, caloric burn, calisthenics intensity tiers, heart rate elevation zones, and high-priority action catalysts.
- **Contrast Neutrals:** High-visibility content relies on dynamic off-white (`#F8FAFC`) for primary text and metric titles, transitioning down to soft slate (`#94A3B8`) for secondary metrics and dimensional units.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Space Grotesk** for numerical instrumentation, high-impact titles, and telemetry milestones with **Lexend** for body copy, navigational anchors, and UI controls.

- Space Grotesk offers geometric tension and technical authority, ensuring split-second recognition during high-cadence runs.
- Lexend delivers optimal saccadic pacing and heightened legibility, ensuring physiological instructions, form breakdowns, and secondary telemetry remain readable under dynamic motion.
- Numerical readouts leverage tabular alignments and slight negative tracking for maximum visual density and immediate scan accuracy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a high-density, touch-first fluid grid optimized for mobile ergonomics and one-thumb reachability. 

- **Column System:** Mobile viewports use a 4-column layout with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) screen margins. Tablet and wide contexts scale to an 8-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins.
- **Rhythm & Touch Targets:** All interactable hit targets enforce a strict minimum clearance of 48×48px. Spacing units scale along a 4px/8px baseline matrix to maintain rhythmic precision across dashboard tiles, vertical workout logs, and horizontal carousel track cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy operates via luminous layering and frosted surfaces rather than traditional heavy drop-shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base canvas: Pure deep slate (`#0F172A`).
  - Elevated surfaces: Translucent container backdrops (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.75)`) combined with a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` to permit subtle background pulse effects.
  - Floating controls & quick-action panels: Elevated dark glass (`rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.85)`) paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`.
- **Specular Bordering:** Cards and floating panels feature a top-lit hairline border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and bottom-dimmed edge (`1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`), mimicking precision-milled hardware edges.
- **Luminescent Ambient Glow:** Active metrics emit targeted, low-opacity ambient halos (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.18)` for running/status; `0 0 24px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.18)` for distance metrics; `0 0 24px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.2)` for active caloric burn).

## Shapes

The interface embraces dynamic fluidity balanced with athletic structure. 

- Core containers, metric cards, and workout track modules utilize balanced 8px (`0.5rem`) base radii, scaling up to 16px (`1rem`) for primary workout widgets and 24px (`1.5rem`) for bottom sheets and floating workout trackers.
- Interactive status tags, metric badges, and primary action buttons utilize continuous curvature or full-pill profiles (`rounded-full`) to differentiate rapid tap zones from informational cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic Trigger:** Full-width or oversized pill shape (`h-14`), filled with dynamic neon (`#22C55E` or `#F97316`), typography in heavy slate (`#0F172A`), styled with intense tap responsiveness (active scale down to `0.97`).
- **Telemetry Ghost Buttons:** Translucent glass surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), hairline border (`#334155`), with text/icon tinted in Cyan (`#06B6D4`) or Neon Green.

### Metric Cards & Telemetry Displays
- Built using elevated glass modules (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)`) with internal padding of `space-md` (16px).
- Incorporates a top micro-label in uppercase muted text (`label-sm`), centered huge metric value (`Space Grotesk`, `metric-huge`), and an integrated delta or heart-rate wave visualizer at the bottom.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Ultra-compact pill components with soft, colored background fills (`15%` opacity of the semantic color) and matching solid text:
  - Running Status: Background `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.15)`, text `#22C55E`.
  - Pace / Cadence: Background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`, text `#06B6D4`.
  - Calisthenics / Burn: Background `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.15)`, text `#F97316`.

### Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Input Fields:** Recessed dark fill (`#090D16`), inset border (`#1E293B`), focusing to an electrified cyan or lime border glow. Placeholder text styled in subdued slate (`#64748B`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** High-contrast geometric toggles. Active states illuminate in vibrant primary green with a crisp checkmark; workout rep selectors use circular stepped counters with animated fill progress.

### Specialized Fitness Components
- **Pace Split Strip:** Horizontal scrollable rail of micro-cards displaying split times, elevation profiles, and colored pace variance flags.
- **Heart-Rate Intensity Zone Bar:** Segmented 5-tier horizontal bar moving from resting cyan (`#06B6D4`) to maximum effort sport-orange (`#F97316`), accented with real-time breathing pulse animations.
- **Set/Rep Stepper for Calisthenics:** Segmented tactile counter with high-friction increment and decrement buttons and bold tactile haptic feedback cues.
